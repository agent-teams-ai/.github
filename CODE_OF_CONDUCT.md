# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free community.
Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent while
checking facts, accept technical critique, and keep discussion focused on the
work and its impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owners. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
limit participation, or ban participants when behavior threatens the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing an Agent Teams
project in public.
